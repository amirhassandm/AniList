About this project

Anime Finder brings the key facts about an anime into one place: poster, score, episode count, airing status, genres and synopsis. Open any title to see its studio, trailer, where to watch, main characters and similar anime. Save favorites to My List, and sort or filter by status and genre. Data comes from the free AniList GraphQL API.
